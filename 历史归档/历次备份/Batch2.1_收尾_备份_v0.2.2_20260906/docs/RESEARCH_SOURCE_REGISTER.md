## Source policy

- Prefer official product documentation and primary research.
- Latest 2026 sources are used where they materially change the product story.
- Older sources remain only when they define a still-current standard or experiment method.
- Never copy external quantitative outcomes into this project’s expected results.
- Re-check beta support pages and business figures before public launch.
- Keep course-era competitor conclusions separate from later 2026 industry updates.
